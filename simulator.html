About this simulator

A rebuild of the Helix Logistics prototype: the flight operations console and dispatcher dashboard a remote operator would use to run autonomous medical cargo flights in shared, low-altitude airspace.

What each panel demonstrates

  • UAS Traffic Management (UTM): shares flight intent, predicts loss of separation up to 3 minutes ahead and issues altitude resolutions; reroutes around pop-up flight restrictions.
  • Fault Detection & Isolation (FDI): compares every motor's current and temperature to a model, isolates the faulty unit and redistributes thrust.
  • Near-real-time Verification & Validation (V&V): runtime monitors check geofence, altitude band, energy reserve, navigation integrity and trajectory conformance.
  • Universal Data Compiler (UDC): fuses LTE, SATCOM, ADS-B, Remote ID and onboard data into one telemetry stream, with failover.
  • Wind estimation: infers wind from the aircraft's own motion (ground velocity minus air velocity) and re-plans speed, altitude and ETA.
  • Emergency landing: ranks reachable landing sites by population exposure, distance and surface type.
  • Vision-based landing: locks onto the landing pad with a downward camera when GPS is degraded or visibility is low.

Aircraft assumptions (Chaparral-class)

  • Hybrid-electric VTOL: 8 lift propellers, 4 cruise propellers, turbine-generator plus battery.
  • Payload 300–500 lb in a detachable cargo pod; range about 300 statute miles (published figures).
  • Cruise 110 kt, battery 40 kWh, generator 130 kW (modeling assumptions; the manufacturer has not published these).
